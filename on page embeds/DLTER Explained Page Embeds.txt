        <div class="rtx3-panel-soft rtx3-trust"><strong>48 fixed statements</strong><span>Each item is assigned to a predefined scale before you answer.</span></div>

        <summary>See the exact scoring method</summary>
        <div class="rtx3-details-body">
          <div class="rtx3-method-grid">
            <section class="rtx3-method-block">
              <h4>Items and scale construction</h4>
              <p>The current instrument is <strong>rt-48-2026-08-04-v1</strong>. RS, EC, SA, AR, CP, and TR each use six statements. BS, FS, and ID each use four. Some statements are reverse-keyed before scoring.</p>
              <p>Responses use a 1–6 scale. The mean for each score is linearly converted to 0–100.</p>
